ABOUT 404//SANITY

404//SANITY is what happens when the noise in your head gets loud enough that you have to turn it into music.

Created by longtime musician, producer, and SouthKings Records founder Ron, 404//SANITY is a deeply personal project built around mental health, internal conflict, survival, and the things people struggle to say out loud.

Musically, 404//SANITY lives at the intersection of nu-metal, alternative metal, and rap metal — combining thick, low-tuned guitars, hip-hop-influenced rhythms, aggressive vocals, melodic hooks, turntable elements, and moments that move from controlled tension to complete emotional release.

But the sound is only part of it.

The songs are written from real experiences: depression, isolation, anger, anxiety, self-doubt, emotional overload, relationships, relapse, recovery, and the exhausting cycle of fighting your own mind. Rather than hiding the ugly parts behind vague lyrics, 404//SANITY puts them directly in the music.

The debut album, Voices in the Static, established that identity — music for the moments when your own thoughts feel like the loudest thing in the room. Since then, the project has continued expanding on that story through songs that explore both sides of the fight: the days when everything falls apart and the days when simply making it to tomorrow is a victory.

404//SANITY isn't built around pretending to have the answers. It's about being willing to say, “this is what I'm going through,” when someone else might be going through the same thing in silence.

Real music. Real struggles. Real people.
